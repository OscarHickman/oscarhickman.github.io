<Section title="Research Focus" :first="true">
  <p>
    My work centres on developing statistically sound, physically interpretable machine learning methods for cosmology and extragalactic astrophysics. Specifically, I focus on:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Simulation-Based Inference (SBI):</strong> Likelihood-free cosmological parameter inference and robust posterior validation.</li>
    <li><strong>Sparse Clustering Estimation:</strong> High-performance algorithms and semi-analytic galaxy formation models (GALFORM).</li>
    <li><strong>Field-Level &amp; Graph Models:</strong> Graph neural networks and field-level inference across cosmic web structures.</li>
  </ul>
</Section>

<Section title="Background">
